💡 String Advisor

🔥 Power & Repulsion

Strings like Aerobite, BG66 Ultimax, or Exbolt 63. Very thin gauge (under 0.66mm). High repulsion, great sound, poor durability.

🛡️ Durability

Strings like BG65, BG65 Ti, or Nanogy 95. Thicker gauge (0.69mm+). Lasts for months, but offers less "bounce" and control feels harder.

⚖️ Tension Guide

Beginners: 20-22 lbs (large sweet spot). Intermediate: 23-25 lbs. Advanced/Pro: 26+ lbs (smaller sweet spot, requires perfect center hit for power).
